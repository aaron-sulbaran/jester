Bank lab

Adds clips to Jester's video bank. Each clip's moves are found here once, so the game plays it with no waiting. Dev server only.

1. The bank folder

Pick the folder jester-deploy/bank. The browser asks once to let this page write there.

2. Add a clip

Pick an MP4 or WebM video. People, animals, balls and cars are found.

      Starter clips

      Free Wikimedia Commons clips with their credits filled in. Needs them in app/public/_clips/. Clips already in the bank under the same title are left alone.

        In the bank

        Pick the bank folder to see what is in it.
