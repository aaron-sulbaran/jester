# Ring Fit Adventure: pose guide in a corner, instruction line at the bottom

**Why captured:** a pose game that never shows a camera feed. The guide is a small illustration in the corner, which is the opposite approach from a full-screen overlay. Image is a small 400 px Wikipedia screenshot, so detail is limited.

**What to steal:**
- Pose card: a rounded white panel top-left titled "Tree Pose" with a flat illustration of the pose and a small arrow showing the direction (ring-fit-adventure-screenshot.jpg). Works for a "next up" hint without a strip along the top.
- Instruction line: a single big italic yellow-and-white line near the bottom, "Slowly to the left...", with a thin segmented progress bar directly underneath. One line plus one bar is the whole live HUD.
- A circular timer (16:46) bottom-left on a dark disc, separate from the instructions.
- The player is an avatar in a stylised world, so the room never competes with the UI.

**What to skip:** the avatar and RPG setting, the exercise-coaching language. The pose card is a corner element, so it does not replace Jester's outline over the camera.
