# Jackbox Fakin' It: the faker as a black silhouette, secrets as labelled tasks

**Why captured:** shows how a hidden role becomes a character on the shared screen without naming the secret.

**What to steal:** a role is a flat black silhouette with only the eyes drawn in (hat, cape, glinting stare), set on a bright full-bleed card with a heavy two-line caption ("THE FAKER IS / STILL AT LARGE"): small kicker line, huge condensed caps line. Jester's "your part" screen can be the same trick: the outline of your subject as a solid black shape with one pair of cartoon eyes, which is both the secret and the joke. Secret instructions are numbered tiny red kickers ("SECRET TASK #1") over a large one-sentence order; copy that two-size hierarchy for "YOUR PART" over "the dog". The result strip at the bottom uses a flat red band with each player as a hand-lettered name plus check or cross marks.

**What to skip:** the grey-white TV-frame bezel and rounded screen corners (reads as retro TV, not our product); Fakin' It's cartoon faces drawn in an outdated 2015 style.
