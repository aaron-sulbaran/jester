# Quiplash and Tee K.O.: votes land as stacking name tags on the winning card

**Why captured:** the clearest reveal and scoring staging in the genre.

**What to steal:** a head-to-head card shows each answer with its author's name in the lower corner and a small mascot; as votes arrive, voter name tags pile onto the card like stacked stickers, overlapping and slightly rotated, and the points number sits in a black circle at the card corner. For Jester: during the reveal each player's cutout drops into the scene one at a time with a name tag stuck on, and score chips pile up as stamped tags rather than a table. Quiplash 3's clay-textured cards (thick rounded paper-like cards with a soft highlight, slight tilt) against a saturated swirl background give tactility; one saturated colour world per round (blue, green, purple) keeps rounds distinct. Tee K.O. reveals with a lightning crack down the middle and one side catching fire, showing that the loser can be visually punished for comedy.

**What to skip:** the "Join the audience" corner badge; the text-heavy prompt layouts; tilted cards on mobile where the safe area is narrow.
