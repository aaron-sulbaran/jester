# Nex Playground: Mirrorama (see yourself inside the game)

**Why captured:** shows what a player's cut-out body looks like when it is dropped into a designed scene, which is exactly Jester's reveal. Official description: "See yourself inside the game as you move, pose and play."

**What to steal:**
- Paper-doll collage look (nex-mirrorama-cutout-collage.jpg): body parts are cut out, flat-shaded and tilted, then surrounded by hand-drawn stickers (bubbles, a frog, lightning bolts, a crayon starburst) on a warm beige ground with a framed painting behind. A person cutout plus loud sticker accents and a flat background reads as a game and hides rough segmentation edges.
- Mixed media is the identity: photographic cutout bodies against flat illustrated shapes. For Jester's reveal, a flat saturated stage colour with chunky stickers behind each player's cutout.
- The kaleidoscope mode (nex-mirrorama-kaleidoscope.jpg) shows mirrored tiling of the live feed; the room stays visible, which is a cheap "effect over camera" idea for a celebratory beat.
- The in-room shot (nex-mirrorama-room.jpg) shows the plain camera-in-room case, which is the thing to avoid on its own: it is a mirror, not a game, until framed or overlaid.

**What to skip:** the kaleidoscope for play (it destroys the outline you are trying to match), and the full illustrated sticker set.
