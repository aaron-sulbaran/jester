# Google Creative Lab: Move Mirror

**Why captured:** the clearest example of "your pose next to a target pose". The experiment is no longer live (the page says so), so this is from the page hero, not the app.

**What to steal:**
- Split 50/50 stage: you on the left, the matched photo on the right, same height, no chrome between them (google-move-mirror-page.png). Clean and legible; a model for a post-turn comparison of the player and their subject.
- The pose overlay is thick rounded limbs in saturated rainbow colours (orange, red, teal, blue, yellow) with round joint dots, drawn directly over the person. Colour per limb makes it obvious which part is matched and looks designed, not diagnostic. Jester could colour the outline by player or by part.
- Plain pale background and a lot of space; the person is the subject.

**What to skip:** the black museum-style header, the monospaced Experiments with Google type, and skeleton drawing as the main guide (Jester guides with a filled silhouette, not joints).
