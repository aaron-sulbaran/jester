# Nex Playground: Elmo Says (theatre stage backdrop)

**Why captured:** a ready-made staging for "everyone's cutout standing in a spot": curtain, spotlights, and a silhouetted audience. Official art with two characters; Jester would put player cutouts in those spots.

**What to steal:**
- Deep magenta velvet curtain behind a purple stage floor, with one soft pink spotlight pool per performer (nex-elmo-says.jpg). Each reveal subject gets its own spotlight at the position where it stood in the clip.
- Dark, low-contrast audience heads silhouetted across the bottom, which frames the stage without competing with it.
- Colour discipline: three saturated hues (magenta, purple, a hot red) and everything else near-black.

**What to skip:** the cartoon characters and the kids' game fiction.
