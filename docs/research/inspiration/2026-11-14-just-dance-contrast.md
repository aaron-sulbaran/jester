# Just Dance: what to avoid

**Why captured:** a reference for the look Jester must not read as. Only a small 2009 Wikipedia screenshot was available (320 px), so this describes the original game, not the current ones.

**Traits that make it read as Just Dance (avoid):**
- A solid white coach silhouette dancing in the middle of a black stage with neon pink and cyan light rays (just-dance-2009-gameplay.png). Jester's outline over a camera is a different job; do not make it a glowing white dancer on black.
- A move-pictogram scrolling along the bottom (and later the top) of the screen showing what is next. The owner already said no strip of upcoming moves.
- Vertical score bars and a rating word ("GREAT") in outlined type in each corner, per-player colours in saturated neon, and lyrics as bottom subtitles.
- Neon tunnel, lens-flare party staging (kinect-party-44663.jpg is a similar look in Kinect Party): the nightclub register. Jester should feel closer to a game show or a toy than a club.

**What to skip:** all of it. Use it as a check: if a Jester screen has a glowing white figure on black, a scrolling move strip, and neon bars, it has drifted.
