# skribbl.io and Codenames Online: name only, then play

**Why captured:** two home screens that get a stranger into a game with a nickname and one big green button. Also two very different identities: skribbl is a hand-doodled pixel mascot world; Codenames Online is a glossy gradient-card UI.

**What to steal:** skribbl's whole screen is one panel: name field, a customizable avatar (little coloured head with arrow pickers and a dice button to randomize), a huge green PLAY, and a secondary blue "Create Private Room". The avatar picker gives identity in 5 seconds; for Jester, the equivalent is each player picking a colour and a silly face that is also their tag on the reveal. A repeating doodle pattern as a tone-on-tone wallpaper (dark blue on blue) gives texture without noise. Codenames' green ENTER GAME with a darker bottom edge and 3D press is a strong primary button.

**What to skip:** Codenames' dense multi-card landing page (app promo, how-to list, banners) and generic gradient glass cards. Skribbl's rainbow logotype is loud; ours should be one custom wordmark.
