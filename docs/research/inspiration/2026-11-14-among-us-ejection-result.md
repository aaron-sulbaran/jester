# Among Us: the ejection scene as a staged one-line reveal

**Why captured:** best example of withholding the answer then delivering it as a single plain sentence. I could not capture the role reveal screen (the store shots are gameplay), so this is the result moment only.

**What to steal:** a cinematic wide scene (dusk gradient in stacked bands, tiny drifting object, silhouettes of the other players in the foreground) with one centered sentence in plain white sans: "Yellow was not The Impostor." The text arrives after a beat, over the image, no card. For Jester: before the final reveal video, a 1.5 s blackout or silhouette beat with a single line such as "Everyone had a part." then cut to the reveal. Foreground silhouettes of the group give "we did this together" for free.

**What to skip:** the slow pacing of the full ejection (about 6 s); the space setting.
