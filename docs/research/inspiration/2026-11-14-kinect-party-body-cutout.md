# Kinect Party (Double Fine, 2012): the player as a cutout in a scene

**Why captured:** the best examples of removing the background and standing the player inside a designed scene, which is what the Jester reveal video does. Screenshots from the Time Extension page.

**What to steal:**
- Two treatments of the cutout. Keep the room (44658 electric suits with space helmets, 44661 pirate beach): real room visible, props and foreground frames added. Replace the room (44663 neon tunnel stage): the player cutout stands on a fully designed backdrop. Jester's reveal is the second kind.
- Foreground framing sells depth: palm trees overlapping left and right edges in 44661, pipes and gauges down both sides in 44658. Same trick for reveal: player cutout in the middle layer, a few foreground props at the edges.
- 44657 uses a soft white vignette with the player in the middle, props (hats, money) attached to bodies. Per-player props and a name or colour tag would let each Jester player read as theirs.
- Effects ride the body (lightning, sparks, boots) so motion itself becomes the reward.

**What to skip:** hat and prop dress-up, the brown living room, and the glow-everything sprawl; edges here are soft and the quality bar is a 2012 console.
