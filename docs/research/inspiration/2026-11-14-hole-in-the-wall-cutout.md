# Hole in the Wall (TV game show): the cutout shape

**Why captured:** the closest real reference for "get inside the outline". The still is a YouTube thumbnail showing the cutout on its own next to a contestant's worried face. Searching returned only thumbnails and full episodes, so I did not find clean stills of players mid-squeeze; do not treat this as a complete reference.

**What to steal:**
- The hole is a bold, saturated flat shape (here a glowing red cutout with a beveled edge on a near-black field): thick limbs, rounded ends, no fine detail. It reads from across a room in half a second. For Jester, outline the part as a thick, rounded silhouette, not a skeleton or a hairline.
- Dark surround plus one hot colour: the wall is the only bright thing, so the eye goes straight to the target. Applies to the outline over the webcam: dim the camera slightly and let the outline be the brightest element.
- The format is read as comedy: the contestant's expression is the show. Leave room for the player's face and reactions in the frame.
- Per the show's format: success is binary (you fit or you splash). Jester's inside/outside overlap can be softer, but a clear moment of "fit" or "miss" feedback is the payoff.

**What to skip:** the helmets, foam wall and pool, and the thumbnail text overlays.
