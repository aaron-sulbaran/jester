# WarioWare: Move It!: pose instruction cards and comic tone

**Why captured:** how a motion game tells you what to do and keeps the mood silly. These are Nintendo Life screenshots; only two were useful, I did not see actual microgame frames.

**What to steal:**
- Instruction card (warioware-move-it-141927.jpg): black field, an orange spotlit pool, a plain mannequin figure at the edge showing the pose (hold stones close to sides, elbows at right angles), and a large rounded title ("Choo Choo") with a short body text. Reads as a pose briefing in two seconds; a plain neutral figure is easy to render from Jester's own outline.
- Tone (warioware-move-it-141925.jpg): thick outlines, flat colours, radiating speed lines, huge subtitles with white text and dark outline over the art. The jokey narration makes setup feel like part of the show.
- Setup instructions are written as a character speaking ("The Voice from above speaks"), which keeps calibration inside the fiction.

**What to skip:** the cutscene story, Wario assets, and the long text paragraph; Jester setup needs one line.
