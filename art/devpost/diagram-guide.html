How a clip becomes a guide

All in the browser. Nobody draws a level by hand.

1

Fingerprint

sha-256 9f3c 81ab 0e57... Seen it

The file's SHA-256. A clip seen before loads its saved analysis: same clip, same guide.

2

Frames

15 frames a second from the first 30 seconds, decoded by Mediabunny on WebCodecs.

3

Find

person 1 dog

MediaPipe finds up to 4 people in every frame. Bank clips also look for animals and balls.

4

Follow

track 1 cut

Boxes link up into tracks. A cut ends them; a person hidden for up to 3 seconds rejoins.

5

Rank or refuse

1Person 1, big and central 2Dog 1, moves a lot 3Person 2, too small

Ranked by size, place, motion and time on screen. A clip that cannot work gets a plain reason.

6

The guide

One per subject. A person plays as a stick figure, a thing as an outline.

Now the clip is a level anyone can play.
